# ChatBucket — B2B AI Multilingual Communication Platform Knowledge Base

## 1. What is ChatBucket (B2B)?

ChatBucket is an AI-powered communication platform that lets businesses talk to
their customers across languages, in real time, across both **text and voice**.

Most translation tools give you raw building blocks and leave you to assemble
them. ChatBucket gives you both: ready-to-use AI solutions a business can
switch on, and the individual APIs underneath that developers can mix and
match for their exact need.

**One-line positioning:** ChatBucket is the **India-first communication
layer** that lets any business serve customers in their own language —
across text, voice, and AI agents — using ready-made solutions or
individual APIs.

**What makes it stand out:**
- **India-first** — built around the languages Indians actually speak and type.
- **Flexible** — use complete AI agents, or just the API pieces you need.
- **Complete** — translation, speech, and AI agents in one platform, not scattered tools.

## 2. Products

There are two ways to use ChatBucket:

### A. Ready-to-use solutions (complete, switch-on experiences)

| Solution | What it does | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Translation** | Converts language instantly so each person communicates in their own language, across text and voice. | 19 Indian + 183 Global |
| **Chat Agents** | AI assistants that hold real text conversations and answer customer questions automatically. | 10 Indian + 30 Global |
| **Voice Agents** | AI assistants that talk with customers over calls and reply naturally in their language. | 10 languages + 30 Global |

### B. Individual APIs (building blocks — use alone or combine)

| API | What it does | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Speech-to-Text (STT)** | Turns spoken audio into accurate written text, including Indian accents. | 10 Indian + 80 Global |
| **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** | Reads text aloud in a natural, human-sounding voice. | 10 Indian + 30 Global |
| **Translation API** | The same translation engine, available standalone to plug into any product. | 19 Indian + 183 Global |

**How they fit together:** A Voice Agent combines Speech-to-Text (to hear),
Translation (to understand across languages), and Text-to-Speech (to reply
out loud). A business can take the finished agent, or use any single API by
itself — e.g. Speech-to-Text alone for dictation, or Text-to-Speech alone
for voice announcements.

### Languages supported for translation

every major script: Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada,
Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia,
Punjabi, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu, Santali, English.

- **Speech-to-Text** covers 10 widely-used Indian languages.
- **Text-to-Speech** covers 10 Indian languages.
- **Chat and Voice Agents** operate across 10 Indian languages (plus Global coverage).

## 6. Terminology notes

- "Chat Agents" and "Voice Agents" are ready-to-use, complete AI solutions —
  not the same as the raw APIs (STT, TTS, Translation API) that developers
  can use individually to build custom solutions.
- Rates differ meaningfully between Global-language and Indian-language
  usage — always clarify which the business needs before quoting a price.
- This is a B2B/developer-facing offering, distinct from the ChatBucket
  consumer messaging app and its ToDoZee AI assistant.

  ## Company & Founder

**Our Story**
ChatBucket's founder, **Dr. Sunil Singh**, traveled widely and noticed that
most countries didn't speak English as often as people in the UK, US, and
India. Translation apps helped, but since they didn't capture emotions
properly and missed important details, conversations felt awkward.

A bigger realization followed: none of the popular communication platforms
were built for people with hearing problems, vision issues, or speech
difficulties. Poor language support combined with the exclusion of people
with sensory disabilities revealed a massive gap nobody was addressing.
That's when Dr. Singh set out to build something better — and that's how
ChatBucket was born: a platform built for **ALL**.
